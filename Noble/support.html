Noble Support

Help for the Noble: World History app

Contact us

Questions, bug reports or feedback? Email perl.b.113@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days. If something isn't working, tell us which iPhone and iOS version you're using and what you were doing when it happened.

Your account and progress

  • Signing in. Noble uses Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In. Your progress, streak and XP are saved to your account.
  • Using another device. Sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account and your progress syncs automatically.
  • Lost progress? Make sure you signed in the same way as before. An Apple sign-in and a Google sign-in are separate accounts, even if they share an email address.

Noble Pro

Noble Pro unlocks every lesson, unlimited energy, unlimited reviews and premium color themes. It's available monthly, every three months, or yearly, and renews automatically until you cancel.

  • Restore a purchase. In Noble, go to Profile → Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the App Store with the Apple ID you used to subscribe.
  • Cancel a subscription. On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Noble and tap Cancel Subscription. You can also get there from Profile → Manage Subscription in the app. You keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for.
  • Refunds. Payments are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Deleting your account

Go to Profile → Delete Account and confirm. This permanently deletes your account and all of your progress. Deleting your account does not cancel a Noble Pro subscription; cancel it in Settings as described above.

A lesson won't load

Lessons download the first time you open them, so check your internet connection and tap Retry. Once a lesson has loaded it works offline.
