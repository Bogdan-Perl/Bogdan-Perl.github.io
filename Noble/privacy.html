Privacy Policy

For the Noble app · Effective date: November 26, 2026 · Updated: November 26, 2026 (website)

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Noble app ("Noble", "we", "us") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Noble you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you create an account we receive your name, email address, and a unique account identifier from your chosen sign-in provider (Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In). If you use Sign in with Apple's private email relay, we only ever see the relay address.
  • Learning data. Your progress through lessons and quizzes, answers and accuracy, streaks, XP, achievements, daily goals, and preferences you set (such as your theme, reminder time, and onboarding survey answers like your interests).
  • Subscription data. Your Noble Pro subscription status and purchase history. Payments are processed by Apple; we never receive or store your payment card details.
  • App usage data. How you use Noble, such as which screens you open, lessons you start and finish, questions you answer and whether they were correct, onboarding steps, and when the upgrade screen is shown or a purchase is made. This comes with basic technical details: app version, device model, operating system, approximate country, and an app instance identifier linked to your account identifier.
  • Crash and diagnostic data. If the app crashes, a crash report with technical details about the crash, your device, and the app's state at the time, linked to your account identifier so we can find and fix the problem.

Noble does not include third-party advertising, does not use your device's advertising identifier, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. Usage and crash data are used only to understand and improve Noble.

How we use your information

  • To provide the app's core features and save your progress.
  • To sync your account and progress across your devices.
  • To personalize your experience (for example, surfacing lessons matching your interests).
  • To manage your Noble Pro subscription and entitlements.
  • To understand how Noble is used (for example, where people stop during a lesson) so we can improve lessons and features.
  • To find and fix crashes and bugs.
  • To send the local reminder notifications you opt into (these are scheduled on your device; we do not operate a remote push server).

How your information is stored and shared

Your account and learning data are stored using Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Cloud Storage), operating on Google Cloud infrastructure. Usage data is collected with Google Analytics for Firebase and crash reports with Firebase Crashlytics. We share information only with the service providers needed to run Noble:

  • Google Firebase — data storage, authentication, app usage analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase), and crash reporting (Firebase Crashlytics). See Firebase's privacy information.
  • Apple and Google — to authenticate your sign-in.
  • Apple — to process subscription payments through the App Store.

We do not sell your personal information.

Our website

This website (bogdan-perl.github.io/Noble) is a simple page about the app. When you visit it, for example from one of our ads on Facebook or Instagram, we use the Meta Pixel to measure whether our ads work. Meta receives information such as your IP address, browser and device details, the page you visited, and whether you tapped the App Store button. It may link this to your Facebook or Instagram account under Meta's privacy policy. We use it only to measure and improve our ads, and we never send Meta anything from inside the app, such as your account details, progress or answers. You can manage how Meta uses this information in your Facebook or Instagram ad preferences. The website uses no other trackers.

Deleting your account and data

You can permanently delete your account at any time from within the app: Profile → Delete Account. This removes your account and its associated data (your authentication record and your stored progress and preferences) and revokes the Sign in with Apple token where applicable. You may also email us at the address below to request deletion.

Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, the associated data is removed as described above.

Children

Noble is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above.
